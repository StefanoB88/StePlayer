# App Review Notes (Apple)

This is a general-purpose HLS/M3U network video player for personal media servers and user-provided M3U lists.
The app does not provide, sell or include any audiovisual content or subscription, and it ships completely empty.

To test playback:
1. Accept the first-launch notice ("Ho capito e accetto").
2. Choose "Lista M3U" and enter this URL (Blender Foundation films under Creative Commons licenses only):
   https://stefanob88.github.io/steplayerwebsite/demo.m3u
3. Open "Live" (HLS streams) or "Film" (MP4 files) and select any item.

Notes:
- All data stays on the device (Keychain "this device only" for credentials, database excluded from backups). The
  app has no account, no backend, no analytics.
- Privacy policy: https://stefanob88.github.io/steplayerwebsite/privacy.html — Terms of use: https://stefanob88.github.io/steplayerwebsite/terms.html
- "Invia una lista alla TV" transfers a list from the phone to a TV on the same local network only (one-time code).
- No in-app purchases, no external payment links.
